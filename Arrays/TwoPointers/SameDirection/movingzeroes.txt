#The Main Idea Of Moving Zeroes

-->Pointer 1 (read)

Reads every element..

-->Pointer 2 (write)

Keeps track of where the next non-zero element should be placed.

------------Important Observation--------------
-->Read Pointer
Always moves.
read++

-->Write Pointer
Moves only when a non-zero element is found.
This is the most important idea in this problem.
