Comparison of the First Three Patterns:
Pattern	            Inner Loop
Square	            j <= n
Triangle	        j <= i
Inverted Triangle	j <= n-i+1



Memory Trick ⭐⭐⭐(pyramid)

Whenever you see a full pyramid, remember:

Spaces ↓
n-i
Stars ↑
2*i-1

Inverted Pyramid
i-1----->Spaces
2*(n-i)+1-------->stars

That's the entire logic.
